
### Goods
Things that are useful in day to day life. 
Example: Food, clothing, books

### Services
A service is something someone provides us with their expertise or skill in a particular area.
Example: Renting/ hiring a cab

### Primary Income
Refers to any earnings directly related to the individual's primary job or business, including salary, bonuses, commissions and wages.

### Secondary Income
It is the additional source of income beyond the primary source which may supplement an individual's earnings.
