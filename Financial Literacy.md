## What is Financial literacy
Financial literacy is the capacity to handle personal finances including:
- Borrowing
- Investing
- Saving
- Budgeting
- Future planning

It helps in:
- Attaining Objectives
- Avoiding debt
- Growing one's money
- Protecting one's financial future
- having more control over life
- being financially independent


## Financial Expenditure
It is divided into 3 main categories:
- Necessities:
	- Necessary for living.
	- Food, shelter clothing, education, transport, Insurance, etc
- Comfort:
	- Provides overall satisfaction and comfort in one's life. 
	-  Hobbies, outings, etc.
- Luxuries:
	- Provides more pleasure beyond current comfort levels.
	- Buying a top-end car instead of a budget car just for showing off.
